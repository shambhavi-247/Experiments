PM (Phase Modulation)
SSB-SC (Single Sideband Suppressed Carrier)
DSB-SC (Double Sideband Suppressed Carrier)
Pre-Emphasis & De-Emphasis Filters
Even & Odd Signal Decomposition
Discrete-Time Sequences

TDM (Time Division Multiplexing)
TDM Demultiplexing
Other fundamental MATLAB signal-processing tasks
